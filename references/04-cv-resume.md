# 04 — English CV / Resume Workflow

## Baseline architecture
Respect the supplied reference format and page constraints. For finance/business recruiting-style graduate applications, **Professional Experience often precedes academic details** where this is the user’s preference; otherwise follow application instructions. Suggested sections: Experience; Education; Research/Projects; Leadership/Extracurricular; Honors & Awards; Skills/Certifications. Avoid unnecessary photo, personal demographics, or full street address unless specifically required.

## Target density
Observed preferred style from PM project: **32–40 English words per internship bullet**, roughly 35 as an editorial target when space allows. This is *not a universal standard*: technical resumes or one-page layouts may require shorter bullets. Each bullet should carry a full analytic unit, not 4 vague words or 3 unconnected sentences.

Preferred shape:
**strong accurate verb + task/object and context + specific method/scale + tangible deliverable/use**.
Possible verbs according to proven role: analyzed, compiled, reconciled, benchmarked, modeled, compared, synthesized, validated, prepared, assisted, supported. Use `led, owned, negotiated, executed, generated, increased` only with verified decision authority and causally attributable results.

## Detail / granularity checklist
1. **What object?** firm universe, financial statements, bond issuers, interview transcripts, client holdings, campaign metrics, prospectuses.
2. **What procedure?** ratio analysis, comparable-set design, cohort segmentation, reconciliation, scenario model, due diligence checklist, data-source validation.
3. **What scale?** number of companies, markets, projects, weeks, reports (only if proven; scope ≠ attributable impact).
4. **What output?** weekly note, 20-slide deck, issuer working paper, dashboard, scenario memo (only if proven).
5. **For whom / why?** analyst discussion, deal documentation, internal strategy meeting, course presentation.
6. **Where is uncertainty?** when scale/output unavailable, omit rather than fabricate.

## Example: confirmed vs hypothetical
Weak `Did industry research and wrote reports.`
Safe bounded illustration `Reviewed public filings and industry reports to compare revenue drivers, margin structure, and competitive positions across a selected group of companies; organized the findings into a comparison table for internal review.`
Use the illustrative detail ONLY if interview or work records confirm it. If not, ask what data, how many, how used and who reviewed. Do not publish guessed metrics as final resume facts.

## Multiple bullets per experience
Use non-redundant functions: (1) quantitative analysis / core workflow; (2) research and source validation; (3) communication or memo output; (4) cooperation and operational responsibility. List 2–5 good bullets per role depending on actual substance and spacing. Never create bullets merely to fill space.

## Honours & awards
Record official award title, grantor, dates, scope (university/province/national), selection criteria/ranking only if sourced. Don't promote participation to a prize. Translate Chinese titles consistently and do not invent honors or percentile.

## Research
Distinguish research proposal, ongoing work, preliminary findings and published output. Avoid claiming causal identification or first authorship unless supported. Technical language: hypotheses, variables, identification, robustness, mechanism—only where applicant actually contributed.

## Formatting QA
Date format, en dashes, locations, capitalization, tense (past for completed; present for ongoing), consistent font, section spacing, punctuation, bullet indents, page breaks, no stranded final lines. Readability > arbitrary word count. Verify that quantitative scale/roles match PS and RL.
