# 08 — Programme archetypes: adjust the evidence, not the facts

| Programme orientation | Lead with | Evidence to prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate Finance / MFin | valuation problem and analytical judgement | accounting-to-cash-flow links, credit/issuer research, forecast assumptions, transaction materials | unexplained finance jargon, claiming deal ownership |
| Economics / Applied Economics | incentives, channels, inference | measurement, policy transmission, econometrics, competing explanations | descriptive correlation claimed as causal |
| Accounting & Finance (academic) | reporting and economic interpretation | specific accounting exercise, standards interpreted correctly, investment relevance | internship list disguised as academic motivation |
| Oxford-style academic-intensive MFE | intellectual curiosity rooted in coursework and economic theory | research questions, methodological curiosity, what programme enables academically | standard IB-to-PE pitch, overloaded internship chronology |
| Management / MiM | organizational constraints and concrete coordination | feedback, dependency mapping, communication, decision support | generic leadership buzzwords |
| Business Analytics / AI | turning messy data into reliable decisions | operational definition, model validation, implementation limits, organizational adoption | confident unsupported AI deployment claims |
| Global Operations | systems, bottlenecks, cross-functional handoffs | process dependencies, supply chain/logistics, service operations | calling coordination “transforming global operations” |
| Family Office / Family Business | stewardship, intergenerational and risk-sensitive decisions | asset allocation constraints, governance, long horizon, trust/confidentiality | pretending to have personally advised HNW families |
| PEEM / Political Economy | mechanisms tying policy, institutions and markets | heterogeneity, regulation, political constraints, economic evidence | pure corporate-finance pitch with no policy logic |
| Scholarship / Contribution | values shown through sustained behavior | credible service, initiative, intellectual generosity | prestige and generic diversity claims |

## Dedicated academic pivot pattern (from Oxford/LSE revision feedback)
When a user asks to foreground academics and cut internship experience, *really change the architecture*: first half: one academic puzzle → coursework/reading/research method → intellectual fit and specific curriculum, without leading with internship credentials. Second half: career direction → specific actions during programme → why the school's environment amplifies those actions. Internship may appear later as one concise supporting fact if relevant; not obligatory.

## Distinct KCL short-answer pattern
For a required writing sample: exact heading per question (can reproduce question in title), separate answers, each sublimit tracked individually, global suggested length if supplied. Do not deliver one continuous general PS.

## Reuse narrative intelligently
One observed instance could be mapped as:
- Finance: why a growth rate alone is insufficient for valuation;
- Economics: whether a policy shift changed actual incentives;
- Management: what information a decision-maker needed;
- AI: whether signal is stable under measurement change.
But **do not assert all four events happened**; the same factual task may only support a limited set of angles. All new details must be verified.
