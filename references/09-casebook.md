# 09 — Anonymized pattern library from PM admissions work

**Not a repository of student records.** These are *transferable editing patterns*. School/prompts are historical working examples, not up-to-date application requirements. Some preliminary “story ideas” were brainstorm hypotheses and require individual reconfirmation before any use.

## Case A — Multi-programme finance/economics/management applicant
Historical work involved KCL Corporate Finance, PEEM, Finance/Economics, NTU Enterprise AI, CUHK ISTM, HKUST Global Operations / Family Office, HKU Economics and NTU MiM.

**Lessons:** One resume can yield multiple correct narrative tracks only when the actual evidence supports them. Economics draft needs mechanism/measurement; Finance needs cash-flow/valuation; Management needs decision-useful communication or team dependency; AI needs reliable data and actionable implementation. Keep the `factual substrate` fixed, reorder emphasis. An operations story about a missed handoff is not automatically a verified organization-wide efficiency gain.

**Application formatting:** KCL-style multi-answer questions may have mandatory individually titled sections and character caps; NTU MiM challenge essay and scholarship prompt are independent outputs. A user-requested ~600 / ~500 / ~850 word target is an editorial instruction, not proof the programme officially prescribes that cap.

**Research recommender:** A research-mentor letter was developed around a proposed GenAI/political connections firm-level mechanism, contrasting hiring composition with net employment and alternative public-contract explanations. The reusable insight: scientific maturity means identifying discriminating observations and competing channels, not claiming unpublished hypotheses are proven.

## Case B — Finance resume + two course-academic letters
Repeated editing request: mimic dense example finance CVs, **internships first**, 32–40 English words per bullet when layout allows, several substantive alternative bullets to select from, and a separately verified Honors & Awards section. Strong detail distinguishes **working paper preparation, issuer data reconciliation, operating ratios, sector mapping and research writing**; counts reflect scope, not execution ownership.

Two professor letters draw from **distinct course evidence**, such as financial-statement analysis versus a separate assignment/research context. A comparative ratio project can demonstrate ability to interpret a business metric, but no professor can automatically vouch for unobserved internships.

## Case C — Accounting & Finance / Financial Economics academic pivot
Iterative feedback emphasized moving away from a strong but repetitive consulting/private equity/strategic investment CV recap. For an academically demanding programme, use a classroom problem (e.g. how financial statement categorization changes interpretation, relevant costing, economic fundamentals) to establish intellectual motivation. Connect the academic puzzle to future valuation/finance judgment. When revising an Oxford-style question, explicitly move half the essay toward coursework/programme fit and the latter half toward career objectives, planned programme actions, and school-specific leverage.

## Case D — Interview preparation
Historical CUHK group-finance practice required short advance-thinking windows, one-minute speeches, subsequent discussion, and occasional personal follow-up. Practice: propose a clear decision framework under uncertainty (e.g. upside versus downside, firm distress, market power, banking innovation or AI in finance), make a recommendation conditional on objective constraints, then acknowledge what evidence would change your view. A requested 100–150-word response can be prepared as oral practice material, not copied verbatim into every prompt.

## Case E — Editing philosophy across all cases
- Strength comes from **specificity of action and judgment**, not artificial heroism.
- “Show the work”: revised assumption, changed variable, reconstructed table, clearer working paper.
- **Distinct genres**: CV documents what; essay explains intellectual progression; referee describes observed qualities.
- Avoid presenting suggested details in historical brainstorm drafts as independently verified facts.
