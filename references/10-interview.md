# 10 — Interview transfer: evidence to oral performance

## Group discussion answer, ~100–150 words when requested
Use `position → criterion → trade-off → illustrative evidence/conditional recommendation → what would change my mind`. At 1 minute, prioritize one or two decision factors, not a tour of the entire topic. Distinguish the initial 1-minute prepared statement from interactive follow-ups; listen to peers and engage rather than repeat memorized paragraph.

## Finance examples as *frameworks*, not preset answers
- Low-risk modest return vs 50% failure / 50% tripling revenue: expected payoff alone does not determine choice; risk tolerance, survival needs, liquidity, cost of capital, and variance matter. For a struggling firm, downside survival and financing constraints increase importance of ruin risk.
- Large investment institution vs nimble competitors in emerging markets: local information advantage, decision speed, compliance, risk budget, operational execution and long-horizon discipline, not just size.
- Slow banking innovation: legacy infrastructure, regulatory oversight, incentive asymmetry, cybersecurity, integration risk and risk of mistakes; recognize valid benefits of conservatism.
- AI task and career: say exactly where AI assists, how outputs are validated, and what professional judgment remains.

## Personal questions
Prepare verifiable 60-second story versions from story portfolio, e.g. an analysis whose initial interpretation failed, a peer disagreement, a team dependency, research contribution, ethical disagreement, and why this specific programme. Interview performance must be consistent with CV/PS/RL, especially dates, scale, technical methodology and exact individual contribution.
